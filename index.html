A practical Wheebuy shopping guide

Turn a link list into a useful shortlist.

Wheebuy finds are easier to compare when every entry uses the same fields. Keep observations separate from claims, and revisit the destination before making a purchase.

Find your next category on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose one category and a spending limit.

    Start with shoes, a layer or an accessory. Set a budget for the estimated delivered cost so that the shortlist reflects what you can spend.

  2. Save the exact product and variant.

    For Wheebuy product links, note the destination, item name, color, size and the date you checked it. Links and prices can change.

  3. Compare fit using measurements.

    In a Wheebuy clothing spreadsheet, record chest, length or waist measurements where relevant. For footwear, check the listing’s sizing method and compare it with a pair that fits.

  4. Review evidence before deciding.

    Read seller information, inspect available photos and check applicable return terms. Leave missing facts marked “unknown” rather than filling them with assumptions.

Suggested fields for your own product comparison sheet.
Field What to record Why it helps
Product & source Item name, exact destination link and seller identity. Helps distinguish similar items and revisit the source.
Variant & fit Color, selected size, measurements and sizing notes. Makes comparisons specific to the version you want.
Observed price Amount, currency and date checked. Prevents an old price from being treated as current.
Estimated total Item cost plus known delivery, fees and applicable charges. Supports a more realistic budget comparison.
Evidence & unknowns Available photos, material information and unresolved questions. Shows what still needs checking before purchase.

Straightforward answers

Before you follow a find.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product-link reference organized into rows and categories. Useful entries include a source link, item description, observed price, size information and notes. A listing alone does not verify availability, authenticity or quality.

Is this an official Wheebuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Its category links lead to CAFANS, a separate website. It does not provide a Wheebuy login, checkout or official support.

Where do the category buttons lead?

The category buttons open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. Other shopping calls to action open the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare product prices?

Record the currency and selected variant, then check any applicable service fees, delivery charges and import charges. Compare the estimated total rather than the displayed item price alone.

Do editorial photos prove which products are available?

No. The photographs illustrate fashion categories. Check the linked listing for the actual item, brand, materials, measurements, seller and availability.

What should I check before using a product link?

Check the destination domain, item identity, selected size and color, current price, seller information, available photos and applicable return terms.
